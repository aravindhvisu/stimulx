Stage 7 — Write & Community Review
Multilingual Stroop interference in Hindi and English
3 reviewers · Community review open
Abstract

The Stroop colour-word interference task has been extensively studied in Western populations, yet normative data for Hindi-speaking Indian adults remains scarce. This study administered a computerised Stroop task in Hindi and English to 178 bilingual adults. Participants showed robust Stroop interference in both languages (Hindi: 72ms, English: 71ms), with no significant difference between conditions.

Dr Sunita Rao · NIMHANS
